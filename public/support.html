Abu Dhabi Outside

When can the kids go outside today? Heat, UV and air quality around the clock.

Help and feedback

For questions, problems or ideas, open an issue on the project page: github.com/Zboule/abu-dhabi-outside/issues.

How to read the clock

  • The ring is today, noon at the top and midnight at the bottom. Its colour is how hot it feels: green comfortable, yellow and orange warm, red too hot.
  • Fire marks hours when the sun is too strong for skin. Violet particles mark polluted air, sand-coloured ones dust.
  • Footprints mark the hours when it is fine to go out, green for good and yellow for OK. A lane of water marks pool time.
  • Tap or drag around the clock to see any hour. Settings (gear icon) set kids limits, pool time and the hours of your day.

Widgets

Long-press your home screen or lock screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Abu Dhabi Outside. The widgets follow the app's settings and refresh about once an hour.

Where the numbers come from

  • Air, past hours: measured at Environment Agency Abu Dhabi stations (adairquality.ae).
  • Air, forecast: FMI's SILAM and NOAA's GEFS-Aerosols for particles, and the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) for gases and further ahead.
  • Weather and UV: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), with UV from CAMS. Contains modified Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information.

Past UV and heat are model values, not measurements, and can be revised a little after the fact.

Not medical advice

The app is a guide for planning time outdoors. It is not medical advice. If you or your child has asthma or another condition, follow your doctor's guidance.
