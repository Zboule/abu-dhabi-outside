Privacy policy

Abu Dhabi Outside, iPhone app and website. Last updated 30 October 2026.

In short

The app collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does

To show when it is a good time to go outside in Abu Dhabi, the app downloads weather, UV and air quality data for Abu Dhabi. These requests ask only for Abu Dhabi data; they do not include your location or anything about you. The data comes from:

  • this website (zboule.github.io), which publishes the Environment Agency Abu Dhabi station readings and the forecasts the app uses;
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), for weather, UV and the CAMS air quality model.

As with any website, these services can see the network address of the device making the request, as part of how the internet works. The app sends them nothing else.

Your settings

Your choices (kids limits, pool time, the hours of your day) are stored only on your device, so the app and its widgets can use them. They never leave your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

The app does not use your location. It always shows Abu Dhabi.

Children

The app is meant for parents and anyone planning time outdoors. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Beta testing

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. The published app sends nothing.
